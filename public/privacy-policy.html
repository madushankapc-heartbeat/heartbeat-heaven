Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how HEARTBEAT HEAVEN handles information in its mobile application and related website/service. HEARTBEAT HEAVEN is operated by Madushanka.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, HEARTBEAT HEAVEN may handle the following information:

  • Account information: phone number or username used for account access, account identifier, password-related security information, recovery-question information, and account/device security information.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as display/profile details and profile images.
  • User content: messages, group content, stories, reactions, reports, uploaded media, and other content you choose to submit through the service.
  • Music and website content: song information, lyrics, cover artwork, audio files, likes and related public website content.
  • Technical and security information: authentication/session information, device-related security identifiers used for account protection, IP/network information used for security and rate limiting, and security/moderation logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate HEARTBEAT HEAVEN, including account login and recovery, profiles, messaging, groups, stories, music playback, likes, notifications, security, abuse prevention, moderation, troubleshooting, and service improvement.

Security-related information may be used to protect accounts, enforce rate limits, detect abuse, maintain service integrity, and investigate security or moderation incidents.

3. User content and private groups

Content you submit may be visible to other users according to the feature's privacy and access controls. Private groups are intended to restrict normal-user access, but platform administrators may access or manage private-group information when reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, security, legal compliance, or service administration.

Users should not submit information that they are not permitted to share.

4. Sharing and service providers

HEARTBEAT HEAVEN does not sell personal information for money. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the service, such as hosting, database, authentication, storage, and infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a valid legal request, protect users or the service, prevent fraud or abuse, or respond to security incidents.

5. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service, including HTTPS/TLS for network transmission, access controls, row-level database security, restricted server-side credentials, authentication controls, rate limiting, and security logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Account deletion and data deletion

You can request deletion of your HEARTBEAT HEAVEN account through the account-deletion feature provided in the app. Account deletion is intended to remove the account and associated user data handled by the service, subject to limited retention that may be necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

We do not retain data longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, except where continued retention is required or permitted by applicable law or is necessary for legitimate security and operational purposes.

An external account-deletion request page is provided separately at Account Deletion.

7. Children and minimum age

HEARTBEAT HEAVEN is intended for users aged 16 or older. The service does not intentionally provide account creation to users below this minimum age. If we learn that an account was created by a person below the applicable minimum age, we may take appropriate action, including deletion of the account.

8. Your choices

You may use the service's available controls to manage your profile and content, block or report other users, and request account deletion. You may also stop using the service at any time.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, technology, security practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact and privacy questions

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about content or account handling, users can contact the HEARTBEAT HEAVEN service through the in-app Owner/Support contact feature. Please include enough information for us to understand the request, but do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

11. Applicable requirements

This policy is intended to describe the service's current data practices in clear language. Applicable privacy and data-protection obligations can vary depending on the user's location and the circumstances of processing. This policy does not replace independent legal advice.
