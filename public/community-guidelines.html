Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Be respectful

Use HEARTBEAT HEAVEN in a way that respects other people. Do not use messages, groups, stories or profiles to threaten, harass, bully, stalk, intimidate or deliberately target others.

Keep the community safe

Do not use the service for scams, fraud, malicious links, malware, credential theft, unauthorized access, or other activity intended to harm users or the service.

Respect privacy

Do not publish or share another person's private, confidential or sensitive information without appropriate authorization. Do not attempt to access private messages, groups, media or accounts that you are not authorized to access.

Share content you have the right to share

Only upload or distribute content when you have the necessary rights or permission. This includes music, lyrics, photographs, videos, artwork and other copyrighted or personally owned material.

No impersonation or abuse of platform features

Do not impersonate another person or misuse reporting, blocking, invitations, reactions, groups or other features to deceive, harass or manipulate other users.

Reporting

If you encounter abusive, unsafe or suspicious behavior, use the available in-app report and block controls. Reports may be reviewed for safety, moderation, security or legal reasons.

Moderation

HEARTBEAT HEAVEN may review reported content and take proportionate action, including removing content, restricting features, suspending accounts or deleting accounts when necessary to protect users, enforce these Guidelines or comply with applicable requirements.

Private groups

Private groups are intended to limit access among normal users. Platform administrators may access or manage private-group information when reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, security, legal compliance or service administration.

Account deletion

You may delete your account using the in-app account-deletion feature. See the Account Deletion page for additional information.
