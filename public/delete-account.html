Account Deletion

This page is the external account-deletion resource for the HEARTBEAT HEAVEN app.

How to delete your account

For the normal deletion process, open the HEARTBEAT HEAVEN app and use its account-deletion option. You may also request account deletion through the app's Owner/Support contact feature.

When an account-deletion request is completed, associated account data is deleted from the service, except information that must or may be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes described in the Privacy Policy.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account, contact HEARTBEAT HEAVEN through the app's Owner/Support contact feature and explain that you need an account-deletion request.
