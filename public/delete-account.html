Account Deletion

This page is the external account-deletion resource for the HEARTBEAT HEAVEN app.

Request account deletion

If you can access the HEARTBEAT HEAVEN app, you can delete your account directly from the app. If you cannot access the app, you can submit an external deletion request below.

Do not enter your password. We will use this information only to identify the account-deletion request.

For account security, this web form does not ask for your password. Requests are rate-limited and reviewed by the service team before account deletion.

When a deletion request is completed, associated account data is deleted from the service, except information that must or may be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes described in the Privacy Policy.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or the request form does not work, contact HEARTBEAT HEAVEN through the app's Owner/Support contact feature.
